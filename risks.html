Risk appetite and treatment

Inherent risk is scored before controls; residual risk is scored after the mapped controls are taken into account. Each residual score is compared with the organisation's risk appetite to recommend a treatment and to flag acceptances that need formal sign-off.

Risk visualisation

The register's likelihood x impact data, two ways. Choose whether to plot inherent or residual ratings, then choose a view — both are computed live from the same data, including any risks added from the threat-intelligence lookup below.

A 5x5 likelihood x impact matrix. Each cell lists the risk IDs scored at that position; the band (score = likelihood x impact: 15+ high, 8-12 medium, 6 or less low) is conveyed by the cell's position and score as well as its colour.

The same data as an SVG bubble chart: bubble position shows likelihood and impact, bubble size shows how many risks share that position.

Live threat-intelligence lookup

This tool checks live security data from trusted sources. It loads recent CVE identifiers from CIRCL, and when you choose one, it fetches detailed information and EPSS scores to help you assess the risk. You can save the result straight into your risk register.

Format: CVE-YYYY-NNNN. Try CVE-2021-44228 (Log4Shell).
