Frequently asked questions

How is a risk score calculated?

Risk score is likelihood multiplied by impact. The prototype groups attainable scores into Low, Medium and High bands.

How do I register an AI use case?

Open the AI Use-Case Register, provide fictional tool and ownership information, choose the data category and oversight arrangements, then submit the form.

Where does vulnerability information come from?

The Risk Register combines a CIRCL CVE lookup with FIRST EPSS information when the public services are available.

Are support requests actually sent?

No. This prototype stores requests only in localStorage for demonstration. A production implementation would use authenticated server-side processing and an auditable case-management platform.
